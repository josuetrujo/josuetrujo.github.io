— How I help

AI help for small problems, big systems, and everything in between.

You don't need to know what tool you need before reaching out. I can help you figure out whether the right next step is training, an AI tool, automation, a custom CRM, or software built around the way your business already works.

01 — AI Training for Teams

Help your team use AI with confidence.

Plain-English training for real business tasks.

I train team members on how to use AI tools for real business tasks — writing, summarizing, organizing information, planning, customer communication, research, and repetitive office work.

What you get:

  • AI basics explained in plain English
  • Tool recommendations for your team
  • Practical examples based on your business
  • Training for daily office work
  • Safe and responsible AI use
  • Simple guides your team can refer back to
02 — AI Tool Setup and Implementation

Get the right tools in the right places.

Choose useful tools and help your team adopt them.

There are a lot of AI tools, and not all of them are worth your time. I help you decide which tools make sense for your business, set them up, and show your team how to actually use them.

What you get:

  • A review of your current tools
  • Finding AI tools that fit your work
  • Setting up accounts, templates, or workflows
  • Creating simple instructions for your team
  • Helping staff adopt the tools without overwhelm
03 — Custom CRMs

A CRM built around how your business already works.

One organized place for customers, jobs, and follow-up.

A CRM is simply one organized place for everything about your customers and jobs. Many CRMs force your team to change how they work. I build practical CRM systems that match your existing process, so customer information, notes, follow-ups, jobs, and schedules are easier to manage.

What you get:

  • Customer and job records
  • Follow-up tracking
  • Notes and history
  • Scheduling or service details
  • Dashboards
  • Simple forms for staff
  • Reports that help you see what's happening
04 — Custom Software and Automation

Software that removes repetitive work.

Automate the repeated steps that slow your team down.

If your team is copying, pasting, typing the same thing twice, manually sending reminders, or updating too many spreadsheets, I can help build software or automations that do that work for you.

What you get:

  • Internal dashboards
  • Automated reminders
  • Follow-up systems
  • Data entry reduction
  • Custom forms
  • Reports and summaries
  • Tools that connect your existing process
05 — Workflow Discovery

Start by understanding the business.

Find what is worth fixing before choosing a tool.

Before recommending software or AI, I take time to understand how your business actually runs. This helps avoid wasting money on tools that look good but don't fit your team.

What you get:

  • Reviewing current processes
  • Talking through pain points
  • Finding repetitive tasks
  • Identifying where information gets lost
  • Recommending what to fix first
  • Deciding whether you need training, software, automation, or a custom tool
06 — Small Projects or Full Systems

Start small or build something complete.

Get help at the size that fits the work.

Not every project needs to be big. Sometimes you only need help choosing an AI tool or training a few people. Other times, you need a full custom CRM or internal system. I can help at either level.

What you get:

  • One-time AI help
  • Team training
  • A small automation
  • A custom dashboard
  • A complete CRM
  • A larger internal software project
  • Ongoing support as your system grows
