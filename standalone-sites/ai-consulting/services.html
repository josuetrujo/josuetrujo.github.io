— How I help

Six ways
I plug in.

Every business is different. We don't have to fit your problems into a fixed package. I'll help with whichever of these areas matter most to you, in whatever order makes sense — one at a time, or a few together.

01 — Where to start

Find What's Slowing You Down

A short, honest look at where time is getting lost.

I spend a few hours with you and a couple of the people who do the work each day. I watch how things actually run — not how they're supposed to. Then I give you a short, clear list of what's worth fixing first, ranked by how much time you'll get back versus how much effort it'll take. No pressure to fix everything. Most businesses start with one thing.

  • A short visit with you and your team
  • A clear list of where time is being lost
  • Honest ranking of what's worth fixing first
  • Recommendations in plain English — no jargon, no sales pitch
  • A short follow-up call to talk it through
02 — Information

Organize Customer and Job Information

Stop digging through spreadsheets and old emails.

I help businesses set up simple, searchable records for clients, jobs, schedules, and the details that matter — addresses, notes, preferences, recurring service needs. The goal is speed: your team finds what they need in seconds instead of minutes, and nothing important falls through the cracks.

  • Searchable customer or client records in one place
  • Recurring details (addresses, schedules, preferences) saved once, used everywhere
  • Simple dashboards instead of messy spreadsheets
  • Notes and history attached to each customer or job
  • Easy enough for your team to actually use
03 — Communication

Keep Your Team on the Same Page

Less confusion. Fewer repeat phone calls.

When the office, the field, drivers, sales reps, and managers all see the same information at the same time, your day gets a lot quieter. I help businesses set up simple ways to share schedules, updates, and job details so the same questions don't get asked five times.

  • Schedule-change notifications by text or email
  • Mobile-friendly updates for people in the field
  • Centralized job and customer information everyone can see
  • Fewer repeat phone calls between office and field staff
  • A shared view of what's happening today, this week, this month
04 — AI

Use AI for Repetitive Work

AI that earns its keep — quietly, in the background.

Most of the AI hype isn't useful for a small business. But there are small, boring tasks AI is genuinely good at — and those tasks can eat hours of your week. I help businesses use AI for the parts that should be automatic, without overwhelming the team or replacing anyone.

  • Automatic reminders and follow-ups
  • AI summaries of calls, notes, or the day's activity
  • Customer lookup and quick-answer assistance
  • Less time spent typing the same information twice
  • Help drafting routine emails, quotes, or messages
  • Simple internal assistants that know your business
05 — Software

Choose the Right Tools

Before you spend money on new software.

A lot of businesses buy software they don't end up using — or pay for tools that overlap. Before you spend, I help you figure out whether something you already have can do the job, whether an off-the-shelf tool would be a better fit, or whether you actually need something custom. Most of the time, the answer isn't "build something new."

  • Review of the tools your team uses today (what's working, what isn't)
  • Honest comparison of options for CRM, scheduling, or communication tools
  • Recommendations with no kickbacks and no upsells
  • A plain-English explanation of what each option would actually do for you
  • A clear answer on whether custom software is worth it (often it isn't)
06 — Modernization

Improve One Step at a Time

Steady changes your team actually adopts.

The best systems are built gradually. I don't believe in replacing how your business runs overnight — that's how teams end up frustrated and the new tools end up unused. Instead, we improve one workflow at a time, make sure it sticks, and then move to the next one. By the end of a few months, your business runs noticeably smoother.

  • One workflow improved at a time, in the right order
  • Short training so your team is confident using each change
  • Plain-English documentation they'll actually open
  • Engagements sized to the work — short or longer-term as needed
  • A steady path forward instead of one big disruptive project

Not sure which one fits?

Tell me a little about your business and what's been slowing your team down. I'll tell you honestly which of these would make the biggest difference — or whether you don't need me yet.
