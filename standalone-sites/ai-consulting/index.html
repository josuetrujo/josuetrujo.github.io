not big promises.

These are the kinds of changes I help businesses make. Small, practical, and useful from week one.

  • Cleaner customer and job tracking, so nothing gets lost
  • Automatic follow-up reminders your team doesn't have to remember
  • Simple dashboards instead of messy spreadsheets

03 — How I work

A simple
rhythm.

Start small. Decide as we go. We agree on one thing to fix, I get it working, and you decide whether to keep going.

We talk

A free 30-minute call. You tell me what's been bothering you. I tell you honestly whether AI is the right answer — sometimes it isn't.

I look at how things actually work

I spend a little time with the people who do the work each day. Not how it's supposed to run — how it really runs.

I build something small and useful

One change at a time. Something your team can actually use within a few weeks, not a six-month project.

I make sure it sticks

Short training, plain-English documentation, and a way to reach me if something breaks. You own the system.
