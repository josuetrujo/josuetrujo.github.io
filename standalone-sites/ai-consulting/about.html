— About

Built by a maker,
for makers.

J

Lumen AI was founded on a simple frustration: most AI advice is written for tech companies, not for the contractors, studios, agencies, restaurants, and family businesses that make a local economy actually work. The result is hype that doesn't translate — and business owners who feel like they're falling behind.

We come from the other side of that gap. The founder spent years in valet operations, in sales and design support, and as a freelance videographer, editor, and graphic designer — running the books, building the systems, shipping the work. We know what it feels like to lose a Saturday to a workflow that should have taken an hour.

That perspective shapes how we build. We don't sell AI strategy. We sell hours back in your week, fewer late nights, better answers for your customers. The technology is the means. Your business — and the time you spend on what matters — is the point.
