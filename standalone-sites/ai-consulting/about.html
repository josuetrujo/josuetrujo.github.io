I'm Josue.
I help businesses
stop fighting
their own tools.

J

Most operational problems aren't caused by a lack of software. They're caused by tools that don't fit the way the business actually works. That's the part I focus on.

I've spent years in the kinds of jobs that teach you how a business really runs — valet operations, sales and design support, and freelance work as a videographer, editor, and graphic designer. I've kept the books, scheduled the work, built the systems, answered the phones, and shipped the deliverables. I know what it feels like to lose a Saturday to a workflow that should have taken an hour.

That's the perspective I bring to AI and workflow consulting. I'm not trying to sell you software. I'm trying to give you back hours in your week, fewer late nights, and better answers for your customers. The tools are the means. Your business — and the time you and your team spend on what matters — is the point.

What I believe

a.

Start small. Ship something.

One working improvement beats six months of strategy decks.

b.

Plain English over jargon.

If I can't explain it to someone who doesn't work in tech, I haven't understood it yet.

c.

Your team is the expert.

I bring the tools. The wisdom about your business comes from your people.

d.

Start small. Decide as we go.

No pressure to commit to a big package. Some clients work with me for a few weeks, others for several months — whatever fits the work.

e.

Steady over flashy.

Small, practical changes your team will actually use beat impressive demos that never get adopted.

f.

Honest about AI's limits.

Sometimes the right answer is "use a spreadsheet" or "hire a human." I'll say so.
