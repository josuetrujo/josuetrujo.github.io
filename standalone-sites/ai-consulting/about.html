— About

I'm Josue.
I help businesses use AI
without
the confusion.

I help business owners turn messy, repetitive, time-consuming work into simpler systems. Sometimes that means training the team on AI tools. Sometimes it means setting up software that already exists. Sometimes it means building a custom CRM or internal tool because the business needs something that fits the way it already works.

I'm not here to sell complicated software. I'm here to understand how your business operates and help you use the right tools in the right places.

I've spent years doing the kind of work that teaches you how a business really runs — sales support, design work, video production, scheduling, customer communication, bookkeeping, and building systems to keep things moving. That's why I care about practical solutions, not impressive demos that nobody uses.

My goal is simple: help your business save time, reduce repeated work, and give your team tools they can actually use.

What I believe

a.

Start small. Ship something.

One working improvement is better than months of talking about what might work.

b.

Plain English over jargon.

If I can't explain it clearly to someone who doesn't work in tech, I haven't explained it well enough.

c.

Your team knows the business.

I bring the AI tools and software knowledge. Your team brings the knowledge of how the business actually works.

d.

Tools should fit the business.

A good system should support the way your team works, not force everyone into a process that doesn't make sense.

e.

AI should save time.

AI should make the work easier, faster, or clearer. If it creates more confusion, it's not the right solution.

f.

Simple is usually better.

Sometimes the answer is an AI tool. Sometimes it's a spreadsheet. Sometimes it's custom software. The right answer is the one that helps.

Where I work

Sacramento, Roseville, Citrus Heights, and the greater Sacramento region in person. Anywhere in the U.S. remotely. Available in English; Spanish on request.
