Full day-trip option

Folsom Lake can be the trip.

Folsom Lake is one of the main choices alongside Tahoe and Lake Alpine, especially if we want more lake time, less reservation stress, and an easier drive home the same day.

Granite Bay or Beals Point

Folsom Lake is near my home, while most of the family is coming from Modesto. We can make this a full lake day with food, shade, paddle boards, the inflatable boat, swimming, beach time, and an easier drive home at the end.

about 2 hr 5 minfrom Modesto, about 95 mi
about 25 minfrom Citrus Heights, about 15 mi
  • Granite Bay Main Beach: good for beach access, group picnic areas, toilets, water faucets, and BBQ stations.
  • Beals Point: good for families, beach time, swimming, fishing, hiking, picnic shade, and campground facilities nearby.
  • Timing: California State Parks notes the main Folsom Lake recreation season is usually May through Labor Day, but beach access depends on reservoir level.

What we can bring

  • 3 paddle boards for calm-water exploring near shore.
  • 1 small inflatable boat for easy lake time if conditions are safe.
  • Best lake setup: choose spots with beach access, parking, toilets, and gentle launch areas.
  • Safety note: everyone should bring or wear properly fitted life jackets, especially kids and anyone using the boards or boat.

Why this option works

  • Easier logistics: no overnight gear, fewer reservations, and simpler timing.
  • Still a real lake day: beach, swimming, paddle boards, inflatable boat, food, and shade.
  • Good family midpoint: doable from Modesto, very close to Citrus Heights, and easier for anyone who cannot camp overnight.
