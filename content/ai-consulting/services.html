— What we offer

Four services.
One outcome:
your time back.

Each engagement is sized to a real outcome you can point to in 30 days — not a vague "transformation." Mix and match, or start with the audit and let the data decide.

01 — Audit

AI Operations Audit

Half-day discovery. One-page roadmap. Total clarity.

We spend 3–4 hours with you and one or two key operators. We map workflows, watch how work actually moves, and look for the high-leverage points where AI saves real time. You walk away with a prioritized, plain-English plan — ranked by impact and effort — so you can decide what's worth building and what to leave alone.

  • Workflow inventory with bottleneck heat map
  • Ranked list of 5–10 AI opportunities with rough ROI
  • Tool stack recommendations (no upsells, no kickbacks)
  • One-page roadmap you can hand to anyone
  • 60-minute walk-through call with the team
02 — Build

Workflow Automation Build

Pick one pain point. We ship a working system.

You pick one repeatable workflow that eats hours every week — proposal drafting, email triage, lead intake, invoice follow-up, content production, data clean-up. We design, build, and deploy it end-to-end using tools you likely already pay for (Google Workspace, Zapier, Make, Airtable, Claude, ChatGPT). We test it against your real work, hand it over with documentation, and stand behind it.

  • One automation, fully deployed and tested
  • Built on your existing tool stack where possible
  • Documentation, video walk-through, and training
  • Two weeks of post-launch tune-up included
  • Plain-English explanation of how it works under the hood
03 — Assistants

Custom GPTs & Internal Assistants

A teammate that knows your business.

We build internal assistants trained on your specific stuff: SOPs, pricing sheets, brand voice, FAQs, past quotes, common client questions. Your team asks it questions or hands it work like they would a junior employee — and it answers in your voice, with your facts. Available as a Custom GPT, a Claude Project, or a private chat interface on your own tools.

  • One custom assistant fully configured and trained
  • Knowledge base of your SOPs, pricing, brand voice
  • Prompt library for the team's top 10 use cases
  • Access controls and update workflow
  • Training session for the team that will use it
04 — Training

Team Training & AI Adoption

Confident humans beat fancy tools.

The best AI tools fail when teams don't trust them. We run live, hands-on workshops — in English or Spanish — built around your team's actual work. People bring real tasks, leave with finished outputs and a playbook of prompts they'll keep using. We follow up with office hours so the skills stick.

  • Two half-day workshops (live, in-person or remote)
  • Custom prompt playbook tailored to your business
  • Available bilingual: English / Spanish
  • Recorded sessions for new hires
  • Two follow-up office-hour sessions over 60 days
