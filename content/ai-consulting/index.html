02 — How we work

A simple
four-week rhythm.

No retainers required. We scope a small, real outcome and ship something useful before talking about month two.

Discovery call

Free 30 minutes. We listen, you describe the parts of your week that feel stuck. We tell you honestly whether AI is the right lever — sometimes it isn't.

Workflow audit

We sit with one or two people on your team and map the workflow as it actually runs (not as it's documented). Output: a one-page plan with priorities and rough ROI.

Build & deploy

We build the workflow, automation, or assistant — and run it side-by-side with the human version until it earns its place. Iterate weekly.

Train & hand off

Your team gets documentation, recorded walk-throughs, and a live training session. You own the system. We stay available for tune-ups.
